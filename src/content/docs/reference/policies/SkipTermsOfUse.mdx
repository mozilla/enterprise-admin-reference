Configure display settings for the Firefox Terms of Use and Privacy Notice on startup.

If `true`, don't display the Firefox [Terms of Use](https://www.mozilla.org/about/legal/terms/firefox/) and [Privacy Notice](https://www.mozilla.org/privacy/firefox/) upon startup.
You represent that you accept and have the authority to accept the Terms of Use on behalf of all individuals to whom you provide access to this browser.

## Values

- Set to `true` to skip the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice on startup, accepting them on behalf of your users.
- Set to `false` to show the Terms of Use and Privacy Notice. This is the same as not setting the policy.

## Windows (GPO)

```
Software\Policies\Mozilla\Firefox\SkipTermsOfUse = 0x1 | 0x0
```

## macOS

```xml
<dict>
  <key>SkipTermsOfUse</key>
  <true/> | <false/>
</dict>
```
